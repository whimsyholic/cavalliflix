Curator notes

160-show Cavalli archive

Video-first, look-aware

The archive now maps 160 Roberto Cavalli fashion-brand shows across mainline, menswear, Just Cavalli, Resort, and Pre-Fall. Verified public videos use direct embeds, and the remaining mapped cards use embedded YouTube search playlists.

Archive mode

Early Cavalli footage often lives across Vogue, Style.com archives, FashionTV, FF Channel, World Fashion Channel, and scattered uploads. Search-embed cards open an in-page YouTube playlist for that exact mapped show title.

Sources

Reference structure was inspired by GallianoFlix. Current season availability was checked against Roberto Cavalli official fashion-show pages, Roberto Cavalli's verified YouTube uploads, Vogue runway listings, and public full-show uploads.
